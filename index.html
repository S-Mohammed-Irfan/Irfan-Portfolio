CRM-AP Enterprise

Customer Relationship Management system

The CRM project is a Customer Relationship Management system used to manage customer interactions, sales activities, and marketing operations in one centralized platform. The system stores and manages customer information such as contact details, communication history, and interaction records in a single database so that the data is easy to access and maintain. It supports lead and opportunity management, which helps the sales team track potential customers and convert them into actual clients through a structured sales process. The application also includes features for managing marketing campaigns, automating routine business workflows, and handling customer support tickets to improve daily business operations. In addition, the system provides reporting and analytics features that help management monitor sales performance, customer engagement, and overall business growth. The CRM system also integrates with external tools such as email services and marketing platforms to support smooth communication and data sharing. Overall, the system helps organizations organize their business processes, improve customer relationships, and increase overall productivity.

Role: Customer Management, Lead Management, Sales Activity Tracking & Reporting modules

ITCPC

Cardamom auction trading platform

ITCPC is a web-based auction trading platform for cardamom, where users can view market prices, participate in trading, manage inventory, generate bills, and handle logistics including shipping and payments. This system is aligned with modern digital auction platforms used in the spice industry, where traditional manual auctions are being replaced with online systems for better transparency and efficiency. We implemented this system using ASP.NET MVC with ADO.NET for database operations, following layered architecture. Controllers handle requests, business logic is separated, and SQL Server is used with stored procedures for performance optimization.

Role: Auction flow, Inventory, Billing & API integration
